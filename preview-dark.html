07

Do's & Don'ts on dark

  • Sky Link Blue (#2997FF) for inline links — Action Blue would disappear on tiles
  • Secondary copy at #CCCCCC — white would be too loud
  • Adjacent darks: micro-step tile-1 → tile-2 for separation
  • Deepen the product shadow slightly for dark surfaces
  • Never use Sky Link Blue on light surfaces
  • No new accent colors on dark — pills stay Action Blue
  • No hairline-colored text — keep body ≥ #CCCCCC
  • Don't lighten tile fills beyond the micro-step system
