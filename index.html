Questions, answered.

What photo resolution do I need?

If it looks sharp on your phone at full zoom, it will print beautifully. Our system checks resolution as you upload, flags anything risky before you pay — and fixes what it can, free.

How long does delivery take?

3–5 working days, nationwide. East Malaysia typically 4–7. Every order is tracked door-to-door.

Can you retouch or restore my photo?

Yes — every order includes free professional retouching: colour, cropping, sharpening. Old or damaged photos can be restored too. Just ask.

What if it arrives damaged?

It won’t — but if it does, we reprint and redeliver free. No forms, no fuss. Love it or we reprint it.

Can I hang it without drilling?

Every canvas ships with a damage-free hanging kit, and our team can advise on layouts for stairways and gallery walls.
