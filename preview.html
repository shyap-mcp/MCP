10

Do's & Don'ts

  • Use Action Blue for every interactive element — nothing else
  • Headlines 600 weight, negative tracking; body 17px/1.47
  • Press feedback: scale(0.95) · focus rings 2px #0071E3
  • Alternate surfaces for section rhythm — the color change is the divider
  • Frosted sticky bars carry price + CTA through the flow
  • No second accent color, ever
  • No shadows on cards, buttons, or text — imagery only
  • No decorative gradients in UI chrome (artwork gradients are content)
  • No weight 500 · no rounded full-bleed tiles
  • Sky Link Blue (#2997FF) only on dark surfaces — never on light
